✋ Air Draw

Your palm is the pen. The camera follows your hand.

  • ✋Open hand: draw with the middle of your palm. Wide open makes a thick line.
  • 🤏Curl your fingers in to make the line thinner. The gauge on the left shows the size.
  • ✊Fist: stop drawing, so you can move without leaving a line.
  • ☝️Point one finger at the buttons at the top to pick a colour, the eraser and more.
