🎤 Voice Draw

Make sounds and the pen draws them. Use your microphone, and try it somewhere fairly quiet.

  • 🎵Hum or sing to draw. Go higher than your normal note to curve right, lower to curve left, and hold a steady note to go straight.
  • 📢Louder makes the line thicker and faster.
  • 🌈Your pitch picks the colour: low notes are warm, high notes are cool.
  • 🤫"Shhhh" sprays paint.
  • 👏Clap, or say "p!", to splat paint.
  • 🤐Go quiet and the pen stops.
  • 🎶Music: press YouTube or File on the top bar to play a song. In Music mode the beat splats, and bright sounds steer and colour the line.
